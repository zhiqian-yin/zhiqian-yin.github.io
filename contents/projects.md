### YouTag — iOS Music Player

YouTag is an iOS music player application built with **Swift 5**. It supports
downloading compatible audio and video files from direct links and YouTube,
extracting audio from music videos, and organizing downloaded music in a local
library.

The application also provides editable song metadata, background playback,
playback-speed controls, and rule-based playlists filtered by tags, artist,
album, release year, or duration.

**Technologies:** Swift 5, UIKit, Xcode, Alamofire, XCDYouTubeKit
